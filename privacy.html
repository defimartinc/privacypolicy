Privacy Policy

DEFIMART ("the Platform") respects the privacy of its users. This policy outlines how we handle, protect, and process data across our multi-vendor campus marketplace.

1 Information We Collect

We collect essential data to facilitate secure campus trade and reliable fulfillment. This includes:

✦
Identity & Authentication: Full name, email address, and optional profile pictures used to personalize your account and identify you during pickups.
✦
Communication Data: Valid phone number, which is required to send order updates, verify transactions, and coordinate physical collections.
✦
Activity & History: Order logs, wishlist items, saved cart details, and product reviews to provide seamless navigation and personalized catalog recommendations.

2 Information Sharing With Vendors

When you place an order with an Independent Student Vendor on DefiMart, we share your display name, phone number, and delivery location with that vendor.

This disclosure is necessary solely so the seller can verify your order and coordinate the physical handover or pickup on campus. Vendors are strictly prohibited from using your contact info for unsolicited advertising. DefiMart never shares your email address or passwords with independent vendors.

3 Transactional SMS & Alerts

We use your registered phone number to deliver order-critical updates. This includes automated SMS alerts when an order is "Ready for Pickup", when a seller accepts your order, or when an item is out for campus delivery.

💬

Transactional Consent: By using the platform, you consent to receive these essential order-status messages. We do not transmit spam or sell your number to third-party SMS marketers.

4 Data Storage & Platform Security

Your records are kept in secure, encrypted cloud environments protected with access keys and strict token verification. We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party advertising companies.

Your information is used strictly within the DefiMart ecosystem to improve order reliability, vendor verification, and system security.

5 Public Content & Reviews

Reviews, comments on marketplace feeds, and public shop ratings are visible to all users of the platform. Please exercise discretion when writing reviews and avoid posting sensitive personal details, passwords, or private room numbers in public review fields.

6 Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data. You can inspect or update your profile details in your settings at any time.

🛡️

Permanent Deletion: You can initiate account deletion directly from your Profile settings or by contacting DefiMart administration. All personal identifying records will be purged upon verification.

7 Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your data on DefiMart, please contact our support team:

✉️

Reach out through the in-app Contact & Support screen, or visit the DefiMart administration desk on campus.
